## Who's Eason Chang

Hey! I am **Eason Chang (Yin-Hsiang Chang)** 👋

I'm a **fullstack developer turned indie maker**. I love to build interesting [cool stuff](/projects)! My goal is to make humanity better with my inventions.

I also love writing [posts](/posts), covering software engineering, web dev, maker, and productivity.

I'm a Taiwanese 🇹🇼, big fan of bubble tea 🧋! I'm currently living in Calgary, Canada 🇨🇦, building products at **[Aburi Studio](https://aburistudio.com)**!

## What I'm up to

[Carol](https://carolhsiao.com) and I run **[Aburi Studio](https://aburistudio.com)**, a two-person studio building focused software for people who value their time and money:

- **[CoreHour](https://corehour.app)** — a minimalist time-boxing app built around the 80/20 rule ([read the story](/posts/corehour))
- **[FireFree](https://firefree.app)** — track your net worth and project your FIRE date ([read the story](/posts/firefree))
- **[DailyWage](https://dailywage.aburi.app)** — a real-time salary counter with a cute corgi companion

By day, I work as a software developer at [Neo Financial](https://www.neofinancial.com/) in Calgary. I'm a software engineer with 7+ years of experience across startups and enterprises, mostly building for the web with React, Next.js, and TypeScript.
