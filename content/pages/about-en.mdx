## Who's Eason Chang

Hey! I am **Eason Chang (Yin-Hsiang Chang)** 👋

I also love writing [posts](/posts), covering software engineering, web dev, maker, and productivity.

## What I'm up to

By day, I work as a **Software Developer at [Neo Financial](https://www.neofinancial.com/)**, a Canadian fintech company headquartered in Calgary.

Outside of work, [Carol](https://www.linkedin.com/company/aburistudio) and I run **[Aburi Studio](https://aburistudio.com)**, a two-person studio building focused software for people who value their time and money:

- **[CoreHour](https://corehour.app)** — a minimalist time-boxing app built around the 80/20 rule ([read the story](/posts/corehour))
- **[FireFree](https://firefree.app)** — track your net worth and project your FIRE date ([read the story](/posts/firefree))
- **Daily Wage** — a wage visualizer

I am a **software engineer** with **7+ years of experience**, specializing in web **frontend** development using modern technologies like React, Next.js, TypeScript, and Tailwind CSS. I also have experience in **backend** development using Node.js, Python, Golang, and **cloud platforms** like AWS. Before Neo, I worked in both a fast-paced startup environment at [GDP Inc.](https://www.linkedin.com/company/gdp-world/) (a FinTech startup) and a big corporate environment at [Trend Micro](https://www.trendmicro.com/) (a leading cybersecurity company).
